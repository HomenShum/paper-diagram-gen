# Per-surface history entry template

Append entries to the relevant history; preserve earlier entries. Create a surface history and link it from README.md when a new surface needs one.

## YYYY-MM-DD — Concrete behavior changed

- User job: who needs what result.
- Before: observed failure and the source that caused it.
- Change: smallest causal fix and compatibility contract.
- Validation: exact runnable command, realistic scenario and observed outcome. Separate synthetic/local proof from provider, production or visual proof.
- Limits: what remains unverified or outside this change.
